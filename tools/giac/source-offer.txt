The archive geokernel-source.tar.gz contains the application's source
and build scripts from the revision named in REVISION.

The bundled Giac JNI library is GeoGebra's javagiac build 70507 for
the platform named in REVISION. Request its complete corresponding
source, including the wrapper, bundled dependencies and build scripts,
at https://github.com/jash90/geokernel/issues/new. The project offers
a copy at no charge to anyone with this archive for at least three
years from distribution and for as long as the project supports this
build. Include the contents of REVISION with the request.

The verified publisher archive is included as giac-70507-source.archive.
For native installers, giac-dependency-source.tar.gz includes the reviewed
GMP and MPFR source trees and build instructions for the bundled desktop
libraries. NOTICE records the SHA-256 of the supplied archives.
